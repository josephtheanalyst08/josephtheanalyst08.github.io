My Journey Into Data Analytics

How I Found My Way Into Data

My journey into data analytics began while studying Computer Science at university. It was during one of our SQL classes that I discovered an interest in working with data. Curious to learn more, I started researching SQL and its role in data analytics, and that curiosity soon grew into something bigger.

I decided to take things further by attending a tech academy specifically to study data analytics. What started as curiosity became something I genuinely enjoy. From learning to organize and analyze data with Excel to building interactive dashboards in Power BI, writing SQL queries, and exploring a little Python, every step has made me more excited about what data can reveal.

Today, I enjoy the challenge of turning raw data into meaningful insights that help people make better decisions. I love what I do, I'm passionate about getting better at it, and I'm confident in the value I bring through my work.
